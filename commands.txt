===== COMMAND LIST =====

--- CURRENT COMMANDS ---

Mechanism control (Serial / MQTT / etc.):

  m2-2 up       — right mechanism: raise
  m2-2 down     — right mechanism: lower

  m2-1 up       — left mechanism: raise
  m2-1 down     — left mechanism: lower

  m4 up         — center mechanism: raise
  m4 down       — center mechanism: lower

  motors up     — all mechanisms at once: raise
  motors down   — all mechanisms at once: lower

--- NOT WORKING ---

  led on        — [NOT WORKING] turn LED lighting on
  led off       — [NOT WORKING] turn LED lighting off
  led <color>   — [NOT WORKING] set LED color

  ! LED control is currently not implemented / not functional.
